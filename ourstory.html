Our Beginning

A Legacy in Every Seed

Our journey began with a single handful of heirloom seeds and a profound respect for the earth.

Decades later, Tulsi Traders remains a family-led cooperative dedicated to organic purity. By resisting industrial shortcuts and honoring seasonal rhythms, we ensure that every vegetable, grain, and herb retains its natural vitality.

Our fields are a testament to patience, yielding produce that is vibrant, nutrient-dense, and deeply flavorful.

What We Stand For

Principles we don't compromise on.

01

Soil First

We measure success in soil health, not just yield — every field is rotated and rested on a natural cycle.

02

No Shortcuts

No synthetic pesticides, no artificial ripening, no compromise on the slow, deliberate pace of real farming.

03

Fair to Farmers

Every partner farm is paid above market rate — a thriving farmer is the foundation of a thriving harvest.

04

Radical Freshness

Harvested, packed, and delivered within 24 hours — freshness isn't a promise, it's a deadline we keep.

Our Journey

Four decades in the making.

1982

The First Field

A single acre and a handful of heirloom seeds — Tulsi Traders begins as a family smallholding.

1998

Organic Certification

Formally certified organic, codifying practices the family had followed for a generation already.

2014

The Cooperative

Twelve neighbouring farms join as partners, united by shared soil-first principles.

Today

Farm to Door

Fresh, organic produce delivered directly to thousands of families within 24 hours of harvest.
