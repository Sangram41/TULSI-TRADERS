Our Story & Craft

Our journey began in 1982 with a humble spice stall and a vision to bring authentic, uncompromised flavors and sacred essentials into every household.

For over four decades, Tulsi Traders has grown into a trusted name for both wholesale partners and retail families alike. We hand-select the finest dry fruits, aromatic spices, and traditional pickles from native origins, ensuring that every batch meets the highest standards of freshness and rich heritage.

Beyond our kitchen staples, we take profound care in curating our sacred collection—sourcing pure, natural honey and devotional essentials like premium agarbatti and authentic attar. Every single aspect, from sorting crisp almonds and fragrant cardamoms to packaging soothing ritual fragrances, is handled with meticulous attention to detail, preserving their natural oils, purity, and spiritual resonance.
